# Military-family intake coverage

Reviewed July 31, 2026. This note documents why the prototype asks the new military-related questions. It is a design and attorney-review aid, not legal advice or a substitute for matter-specific research.

## Coverage added

- Current and former service status, branch, service dates, pay grade, claimed state of legal residence, and service-order impacts.
- Deployment, mobilization, temporary-duty, and permanent-change-of-station facts that may affect parenting or a court appearance.
- Military basic pay, BAH/BAS, special and incentive pay, retired pay, CRDP, CRSC, VA disability compensation, and TSP distributions as separate financial entries.
- Active, Reserve, National Guard, disability, and deferred military-retirement interests.
- Uniformed-services and civilian TSP accounts, without account numbers.
- Survivor Benefit Plan and Reserve Component Survivor Benefit Plan coverage and election records.
- Transferred Post-9/11 GI Bill benefits, TRICARE/DEERS, and military-connected life-insurance or survivor benefits.
- Secure-document categories for LES, RAS, retirement orders and estimates, Reserve/Guard points statements, TSP, VA, CRDP/CRSC, SBP/RCSBP, and military orders.
- Staff-only routing flags for SCRA, MIL-100, military parenting, military income, and specialist retirement-order review.

## Primary-source basis

- [California Family Code § 3047](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=3047.) addresses deployment, mobilization, temporary duty, parenting-order changes, court participation, and UCCJEA treatment.
- [California MIL-100](https://selfhelp.courts.ca.gov/jcc-form/MIL-100) provides notice of a party's military, veteran, Reserve, or active status.
- [California SUR-230(A)](https://selfhelp.courts.ca.gov/jcc-form/SUR-230%28A%29), effective July 1, 2026, addresses a servicemember's declaration and conditional SCRA waiver for certain settlements or stipulated judgments entered in the servicemember's absence.
- [U.S. Department of Justice SCRA summary](https://www.justice.gov/crt/servicemembers-civil-relief-act-summary) identifies protections involving civil proceedings and other obligations.
- [DFAS USFSPA guidance](https://www.dfas.mil/garnishment/usfspa/legal/) distinguishes a state-court award from DFAS enforcement and explains the federal direct-payment framework.
- [DFAS CRDP/CRSC guidance](https://www.dfas.mil/retiredmilitary/disability/payment/) explains that CRDP and CRSC are separate entitlements associated with retired pay and VA disability compensation.
- [DFAS LES guidance](https://www.dfas.mil/militarymembers/payentitlements/les/) identifies the Leave and Earnings Statement as the central record of earnings, withholding, leave, and allotments.
- [TSP Court Order Center](https://qoc.rk.tsp.gov/qoc/b/CsHome010Home.htm) treats a Retirement Benefits Court Order as the mechanism for a qualifying division related to divorce, separation, or another domestic-relations proceeding.
- [VA transferred Post-9/11 GI Bill guidance](https://benefits.va.gov/gibill/post911_transfer.asp) explains transferred benefits for qualifying spouses and dependent children.
- [California Family Code § 2337](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=2337.) expressly addresses preserving retirement, survivor, and death-benefit interests in certain status-only dissolution proceedings.

## Required attorney validation before production

Heather should approve the question wording, routing urgency, document list, retention rules, and staff playbook. No answer should automatically decide SCRA protection, jurisdiction, income treatment, property characterization, benefit divisibility, custody consequences, or an order deadline.
